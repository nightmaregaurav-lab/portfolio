Blank Template

The Blank Template is a minimal boilerplate provided for demonstration purposes. It comes with no pre-built styles or components — just a clean slate ready for your ideas.

Use it as a starting point to build your own design from scratch. Whether you prefer Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, or hand-written styles, this template gives you full freedom to create exactly what you envision.
