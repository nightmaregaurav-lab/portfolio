Default Template

A fully-featured portfolio template, ready to customize and make yours.

The Default Template is the initial design for this portfolio project. It provides a complete, polished starting point with a cohesive design system — so you can focus on your content instead of building from scratch.

What's Included

🏠 Home

Hero section with cover image, name, and bio — the first impression visitors see.

👤 About

Personal introduction page to share your story and background.

🛤️ Journey

Timeline view to showcase your career path, education, and milestones.

📁 Projects

Project gallery with detail pages to display your work and case studies.

🧠 Skills

Visual skill showcase to highlight your technical and professional abilities.

💼 Services

Services page to offer and describe what you can do for clients.

💬 Testimonials

Social proof section featuring client and colleague endorsements.

🖼️ Gallery

Photo collections with lightbox support for visual portfolios.

📝 Blogs

Blog listing and detail pages for sharing articles and thoughts.

Design Features

  • Sidebar Navigation — Collapsible left panel with profile picture, social links, and configurable menu items.
  • Accessibility First — Skip-to-content links, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation, and semantic HTML throughout.
  • Responsive Design — Adapts seamlessly from mobile to desktop screens.
  • Configurable Content — All content driven by configuration files, not hardcoded — easy to personalize.
  • Quick Links — Custom quick link section in the sidebar for important external resources.
  • Go to Top Button — Convenience button for long pages.
  • Image Lightbox — Click-to-expand image viewer for profile pictures and gallery items.

Everything is built with Next.js and styled with CSS modules, keeping performance and maintainability in mind. The template is designed to be extended — add new pages, customize styles, or integrate new features as your needs grow.
