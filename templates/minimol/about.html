Minimol

A minimal portfolio template that lets the content lead.

Geist Mono, a white canvas, and a restrained type scale give your work a clear hierarchy. Spacing and readable line lengths keep the focus on what you have to say.

Design approach

  • Light body text, considered headings, and quiet metadata.
  • Consistent white cards with subtle borders and compact descriptions.
  • Simple navigation and responsive layouts.
  • Understated controls, visible keyboard focus, and image lightboxes.

What’s included

Home, About, Journey, Projects, Skills, Services, Testimonials, Gallery, and Blog, including project, photo collection, and article detail pages.

Home, About, Journey, and Testimonials have their own Minimol layouts. Other pages use the shared portfolio components with the same typography and card styling.

Using Minimol

Select Minimol under Setup → Core, then manage your content through the existing Setup pages. The template uses the portfolio’s existing configuration, so your content stays independent of its presentation.
